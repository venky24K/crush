Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Welcome to Crush ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe, transparent, and respectful experience on our platform.

Core Commitment: Crush does not sell your personal data to third parties. Your photos, matches, and chats are encrypted and used solely to facilitate authentic dating connections.

1. Age Requirement

Crush is strictly for individuals who are at least 18 years of age (or the age of legal majority in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: When you sign in via Google or Apple, we receive your email address, unique identifier, and public profile name.
  • Profile Information: Your chosen name, birth date (for age verification), gender, dating preferences, occupation, education, passions/interests, and bio.
  • Photos: Photos uploaded directly from your camera roll or camera to build your dating profile.
  • Matching & Messaging Activity: Swipes, matches, and real-time messages exchanged with your mutual matches.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Crush user profile.
  • To power our discovery algorithm and suggest compatible matches nearby.
  • To enable private real-time chat between mutual matches.
  • To enforce community safety guidelines, prevent spam, fraud, and abusive behavior.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored in secure cloud database instances managed via Supabase, with industry-standard TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Storage buckets for profile avatars are publicly accessible via cryptographic URLs only for app rendering.

5. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You have full ownership of your data. You may permanently delete your Crush account at any time directly within the app:

  • Go to My Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  • Upon confirmation, your profile, photos, matches, swipes, and messages are permanently purged from our database immediately.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data privacy rights, please contact our support team at:
